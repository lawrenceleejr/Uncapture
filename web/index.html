Uncapture needs to see your Photos library. It runs only on this Mac and uploads nothing.

Uncapture isn't allowed to see your photos. Turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos, then come back here.

macOS asks again after each update of Uncapture: the permission belongs to the app's signature, and an update has a new one.

Before the first delete

macOS will ask you to confirm. Nothing is deleted until you do.

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days, on every device on your iCloud account. No app can empty it: to free the iCloud space now, empty it in Photos.

Live Photos and RAW+JPEG pairs go as a whole, with their edits.
